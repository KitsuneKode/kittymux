# kittymux mascot — directions and prompts

Claude Code cannot generate images, so these are ready to paste into an image model (best: **GPT Image 2**;
also Nano Banana Pro, Nano Banana 2, Seedance 5.0 Pro). Generate **each prompt once, as its own 1:1 image**
(~1536×1536) — do not ask for a sheet — and keep every result; pick by eye afterwards. Method: the
`ip-as-logo` skill (simplest possible cute IP, recognisable at 32×32, three semantic colours: two for the character, one background).

## The product, in one paragraph (what the mascot must stand for)
kittymux turns the kitty terminal into a calm workspace for running many AI coding agents at once: a sidebar
that shows which agent is working, which needs you, which is out of quota, and which has finished; one
notification per event; one key to jump to the agent that is asking. Audience: developers living in the terminal.
Feeling: friendly, attentive, quietly competent — a companion that watches the herd so you don't have to.

## Three directions
- **A · Kitten** — the "kitty" in kittymux; the friendly host — big round head, two blunt ears, face split into two colours (two panes).
- **B · Owl chick** — attention: it notices when you are needed — round body, one broad rounded brow, two big eyes.
- **C · Baby octopus** — many agents at once, one calm head — round head with three short blunt arm stubs.

Variants: `A1 A2 B1 B2 C1 C2`; odd numbers emerge from the **lower-left**, even from the **lower-right**.

## Prompts (copy one block per image)

> Constraints are written into each prompt (modern models). The words "logo/icon/mark" are deliberately absent.

### A1 — Kitten, lower-left · cream + tangerine on muted dusty blue
```text
Create one complete full-bleed 1:1 square image.
Background: fill the entire square with solid muted dusty blue. Keep the dusty blue visible in every open area and in the corners not occupied by the character; the lower-left corner must be occupied by the character.
Subject: place one extremely simplified, cute, endearing kitten character on the background, reduced to one soft rounded continuous silhouette and one defining feature: two large blunt rounded ears.
Complexity: use only 4–7 large basic shapes and at most two broad internal color regions. Use two simple eyes and one tiny mouth. Remove every nonessential line, outline, anatomical detail, texture, and decoration. Keep the character readable at 32 × 32.
Color behavior: use exactly three semantic colors in the complete image: exactly two character colors, warm cream and tangerine orange, plus the dusty blue background. Make the face two broad halves, cream on one side and tangerine on the other, and reuse tangerine or cream for the eyes and mouth. Keep the character, its facial marks, and the background clearly separated.
Composition: keep the character upright and emerging from the lower-left, filling about 85–95% of the square so it remains visually dominant. Cropping at the bottom or left side is welcome. Show both ears. Never center or bottom-center the character.
Style: make simplification, cuteness, and lovable baby-like appeal the strongest qualities. Use large soft forms, compact proportions, thick rounded contours, and an ultra-clean graphic treatment. Prefer one clear shape over several explanatory details. Add an extremely, extremely subtle, almost imperceptible sense of depth through a barely-there neo-skeuomorphic treatment.
Finish: show only the character on the full-canvas background, with clean surfaces and normal square outer corners.
Constraints: Use no text or watermark. Add no borders, frames, cards, or presentation masks. Include one character only, with no extra subjects or scenery. Use no fragile lines, sharp tips, unnecessary outlines, tiny details, or decorative marks. Add no photorealistic material, dramatic bevel, glossy hotspot, deep occlusion, extrusion, strong three-dimensional rendering, or external cast shadow. Keep the background solid and uniform, with no texture, vignette, or lighting variation.
```

## After you pick one
1. Save it as `assets/brand/mascot.png` (square; keep the native size).
2. `python3 tools/build-brand.py` — writes the 512/256/128/64/32 px versions, a 32 px readability check and a black-silhouette check.
3. `python3 tools/build-notify-icons.py` — every agent's notification icon gets a small round mascot badge, and
   `assets/notify/kittymux.png` becomes the icon for kittymux's own notifications.
4. Where the mascot then goes: README header (use the 256 px), the deck/panel empty state, `kittymux demo` banner,
   GitHub social preview (1280×640: mascot on its background colour + the wordmark you set in your own type),
   the AUR/Arch package icon, and — traced to a single-colour silhouette — one glyph in the icon font for the bar header.
